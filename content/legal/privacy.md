This Privacy Policy explains what information The Savvy Rabbit collects and how it is used. The Savvy Rabbit is published by BH Base LLC ("we", "us"). It applies to our content wherever it appears, including thesavvyrabbit.com, our social media accounts, and any email we send.

## The short version

We don't ask you to sign up, log in, comment, or give us your name or email address to read our stories. We don't sell personal information. We don't run advertising trackers on our website.

## What we collect

**Information you send us.** If you contact us, we receive whatever you choose to include, such as your name, email address and message. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

**Basic technical data.** Our website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. It needs this to deliver pages and to protect the site from attacks. Cloudflare's own privacy policy explains how it handles this data: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

**Analytics.** We do not currently use analytics on our website. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy before doing so and say which service we use.

## Cookies

Our website does not set cookies. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie in some situations, for example to check that a visitor is not an automated bot.

## Share buttons and links to other sites

Our share buttons are plain links. Nothing is sent to Facebook, X or your email app unless you click one. Once you click, or follow any link to another website, that site's own privacy policy applies, not ours.

## Social media

When you interact with our posts on social media, the platform's own privacy policy applies. We may see what the platform shows any page owner, such as public comments, reactions and summary statistics.

## How long we keep information

We keep messages you send us for as long as needed to respond and for our records, and no longer than necessary.

## Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will respond to requests as the law requires.

## Children

Our content is written for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted on this page.
