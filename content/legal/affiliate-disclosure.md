## How we may earn money

Some links in our articles, posts and emails may be affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a commission, at no additional cost to you. We may also publish sponsored content, or receive products or services for free to review.

## How you'll know

Whenever an article contains affiliate links, sponsored content or a free product, we say so clearly at the top of that article, before any of those links. On social media, we label paid or affiliate posts using the platform's disclosure tools or a clear note such as #ad or #affiliate.

## What doesn't change

A commission never changes what our articles say. Our stories are checked twice against their sources and approved by an editor whether or not they contain affiliate links. We only recommend something when we believe it's worth your attention, and our health and money articles never recommend specific investments or promise health results.
