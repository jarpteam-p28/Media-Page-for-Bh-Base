## Health and wellness

Our health articles report what a trend claims and what the evidence shows. They are not medical advice, and they are not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a doctor or other qualified health professional. Never stop, change or replace a prescribed medication or treatment because of something you read here. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before starting any supplement, diet or remedy, especially if you are pregnant, have a health condition, or take other medicines. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

## Money

Our money articles are not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. We never recommend buying or selling specific investments. Prices, rates and rules change quickly. Check current figures with the official source and consider speaking with a qualified professional about your own situation.

## Dating and relationships

Our relationship articles are educational and are not therapy, counseling or professional advice. If you are in an unsafe or abusive relationship in the United States, contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233, or call 911 in an emergency.

## Viral and human-interest stories

We report what named, credible outlets have published, and we say when something is unconfirmed. Stories can develop after we publish them.
