A goal scored by the former Arsenal midfielder Mesut Özil is circulating online again, this time in AI-made videos in which cartoon and film characters take Özil's place and re-enact the goal. [Fox Sports México](https://www.foxsports.com.mx/2026/10/16/mitico-gol-de-mesut-ozil-revive-gracias-a-la-ia-chavo-del-8-woody-y-sherk-toman-su-lugar/) names Woody from *Toy Story*, El Chavo del 8 and Shrek, and [Sopitas](https://www.sopitas.com/?p=1959836) refers to versions featuring SpongeBob (Bob Esponja), Shrek and El Chavo del 8. The trend also drew [a piece from The Athletic](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7696023/2026/10/16/mesut-ozil-ai-goal-woody-spongebob/), the sports site owned by [The New York Times Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Athletic), published on Oct. 16, 2026 under the headline "Woody from Toy Story, SpongeBob and why a Mesut Ozil goal is going viral again (sort of)". It lists Shrek, SpongeBob SquarePants, Walter White from *Breaking Bad* and Uncle Phil from *The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air* among the characters shown scoring the goal.

The goal is the one Özil scored for Arsenal away at Ludogorets in the Champions League on 1 November 2016, the winner in a 3-2 victory, according to Fox Sports México and Sopitas. [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11670/10641000/ludogorets-2-3-arsenal-arsene-wengers-side-fight-back-from-two-goals-down-to-book-place-in-last-16) reported at the time that Arsenal had come back from two goals down, and that three minutes from time Özil lifted the ball over goalkeeper Milan Borjan, went round two defenders and slid it into an empty net. The reason for the revival, beyond the AI videos themselves, is not confirmed here.

## Who is Mesut Özil, and why would one of his goals travel?

Özil is a playmaker born in Gelsenkirchen, Germany, with Turkish roots, according to [Anadolu Agency](https://aa.com.tr/en/sports/profile-world-class-player-at-fenerbahce-mesut-ozil/2122130). He won the 2014 World Cup with Germany and played in the final against Argentina, according to [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/soccer/2014/07/14/germany-argentina-world-cup-final-tactics). He played for Schalke 04, Werder Bremen, Real Madrid, Arsenal, Fenerbahçe and İstanbul Başakşehir, and announced his retirement on 22 March 2023, [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/soccer/news/mesut-ozil-retires-former-arsenal-real-madrid-and-germany-star-announces-its-time-to-leave-the-big-stage) reported. Anadolu Agency reported that he joined Arsenal in 2013 and left for Fenerbahçe in January 2021.

He was known mainly as a playmaker who created chances for others, which can make his own goals stand out.

## What does "going viral again (sort of)" mean?

The phrase suggests the clip is not spreading as it did the first time. Old sports footage often resurfaces when someone reposts it with a new caption, soundtrack or joke. The "sort of" in the headline hints at a partial, niche or lopsided revival. That is a reading of the wording, not a confirmed fact about the posts.

## Why Woody and SpongeBob?

Cartoon characters are a common template for internet jokes because readers recognize them instantly. In this case, according to Fox Sports México and Sopitas, the characters are not shown next to the clip: AI tools swap them in for Özil, so they appear to dribble past the defenders and score the goal themselves. Sopitas reports that people make them with a CapCut template tied to a TikTok trend, or with paid AI sites.

## Is the claim that the goal is "everywhere" accurate?

No view counts, share totals or platform figures have been confirmed for this draft, so none are given. The only established point is that several outlets considered the trend notable enough to explain. Anything beyond that is unconfirmed.
